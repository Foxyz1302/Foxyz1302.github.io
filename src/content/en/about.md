I make apps with Flutter, mostly for the desktop and also for Android. Everything I know I taught myself. I code because I like making things, and most of my apps started as tools for my own day: something annoyed me, so I built the fix.

My current app, **Starsync**, lets friends watch videos together in sync in a real video player. A group of friends uses it every day on Linux and Windows, with its own server, relay, chat and self-updater.

I care most about how an app feels to use. It should be fast and clear, never nag, and never ask for an account it doesn't need. Right now I'm finishing the apps on my list and taking small commissions alongside them. If you need something built, I'd love to hear about it.
