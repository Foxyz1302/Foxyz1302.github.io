I'm a solo developer who builds apps with Flutter, mostly for the desktop and
also for phones. I like software that is fast, looks good and respects the
person using it: no nagging, no accounts where none are needed.

My current app, **Starsync**, lets friends watch videos together in sync in a
real video player. It runs every day for a group of friends on Linux and
Windows, with its own server, relay, chat and self-updater.
