## What I can build for you

- **Desktop apps** for Linux and Windows: tools, media apps, clients for your service.
- **Mobile apps** for Android (and iOS) from the same Flutter code.
- **Back ends in Dart** when the app needs one: sync servers, WebSocket protocols, small APIs.
- **Fixes and features** in an existing Flutter project.

## How it works

1. **Talk.** You describe the idea; I ask questions until the scope is clear.
2. **Written scope.** A short document: what gets built, what does not, milestones, revision rounds, price.
3. **Deposit.** 30–50 % up front, through a payment channel that keeps a record.
4. **Milestones.** You see working demo builds as it grows and pay per milestone.
5. **Delivery.** Final builds and source code after the final payment.
