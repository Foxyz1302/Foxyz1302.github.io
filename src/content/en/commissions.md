## How it works

1. **Talk.** You describe the idea; I ask questions until the scope is clear.
2. **Written scope.** A short document: what gets built, what does not, milestones, revision rounds, price.
3. **Payment in steps.** Small tasks are paid up front. Bigger projects: 30% to start, then each milestone is paid when you have seen it working.
4. **Milestones.** You get a working demo build at every step, so you always know where the project is.
5. **Delivery.** Final builds and the source code once everything is paid.

So you only pay for work you have seen, and the final code comes once everything is paid.
